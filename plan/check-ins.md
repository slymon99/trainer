I've seen a plan I'll rationalise the answer to fit it:

> *If I deleted this week's hard sessions right now — disappointed, neutral, or

power say. The physiological markers detect accumulated fatigue; none of them detects
a fading reason to train, and that is what ended the last block. Through May–June
