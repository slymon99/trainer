# Block roadmap and test schedule

Targets are `%FTP` throughout, against whatever is in intervals.icu sport settings at
the time. Each block's test re-anchors that number, so the same rung means more watts in
each successive block — that *is* the progression between blocks. Inside a block, the
rung moves and the watts don't.

**Firm through Mon 7 Sep. Everything after that is provisional** and depends on a
decision that is deliberately not being made yet (below).

## The open decision: what Sep 8 – 24 is for

It's a ~2.5-week window with about five quality slots. It opens the day after a travel
day and ends into ten days off, so it can't be a normal 4-week block and there isn't
room to ramp anything conventionally. **Decide it after the 5 Sep re-anchor, on the
evidence that produces.** The calendar is intentionally empty until then.

**A — finish the sweet spot ladder.** Rungs 7 (3×20), 8 (2×30), 9 (1×60) at the new FTP,
with room for repeats.

- Rungs 6–9 are **all 60 min in band**, so the FTP bump and the rung progression don't
  compound — no rule broken, and the block progresses by fragmentation alone. That is a
  real answer to "what is a 2.5-week stub block for", which the alternative shapes lack.
- Post-vacation re-entry then opens at threshold rung 1 (3×10 @ 97%), a genuinely small
  session — which satisfies "don't ramp hard the week after the vacation" for free.
- **Cost: threshold slips to 5 Oct**, leaving ~10 weeks of it before the December test
  rather than ~14. `plan.md`'s diagnosis says threshold is the limiter, so this is the
  real price.

**B — start the threshold ladder.** Rungs 1–3 (3×10, 3×15, 5×10 @ 97%), abandoning the
sweet spot ladder at rung 6.

- Threshold gets its full run at the limiter, and starts from a defensible rung rather
  than the 2×20 the old roadmap opened with.
- **Cost: sweet spot rungs 7–9 never get ridden** — stopping one rung past where the
  same progression stalled in May 2026, which is the specific failure this rework exists
  to fix.

**C — one ladder per quality day.** Tuesday takes threshold rungs 1–3; Thursday
continues sweet spot rungs 7–8.

- Maps onto the roles the two days already have: Tuesday is the anchor that never moves,
  Thursday is the droppable one. Dropping Thursday for a group ride pauses only the
  lower ladder.
- **Cost: less legible**, and it breaks the "never jump ladders mid-block" rule as
  written, so the rule would need rewriting rather than quietly bent.

## Test schedule

| When | Test | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Sat 5 Sep** (end Block 1) | **Wachusett**, segment 16244804 — ~24 min max, 2h20 into the ride | The block test and the leaderboard attempt are one effort. A ~24-min max is a better eFTP input than a 20-min. Detail in [`block-1.md`](block-1.md). |

Worth running regardless: it's a controlled reference comparable to January and December, and the gap between it and the Wachusett number is a direct read on durability. Decide on 6 Sep. |
| **Late Oct** | **Alpe du Zwift**, same protocol as January, plus a **4-min max** on a separate day | Alpe is the best anchor — directly comparable to January. The 4-min shows whether the fractional-utilization ratio is moving. |
| **Late Dec / early Jan** | **Alpe du Zwift** — the 288 W attempt | Same protocol, same setup, cool. At 288 W it takes ~47–48 min, still solidly FTP territory. |

Alpe du Zwift as the recurring anchor is a genuine asset: a repeatable ~50-min max
effort in controlled conditions with a January reference. Same fans, same fuelling, same
time of day, every time.

**If the October 4-min max has dropped below ~350 W** while FTP is climbing, the VO2 dose
has been too low — add a VO2 session per fortnight thereafter. If it held near 365 W, the
plan is working as diagnosed.

## Two things to carry into whatever comes next

**Threshold rung 7 (1×60 @ 97%) is a benchmark, not a weekly session.** At 97% for an
hour it is functionally an FTP validation. The other ladders have no such problem — 1×60
at 90% and 1×90 at 85% are ordinary hard sessions.

**The late-December 288 attempt is not fixed.** "Hold it, or slide to January" is a
decision to be made at the late-October test, on the evidence it produces. Naming it now
means it can't ambush the plan later.
