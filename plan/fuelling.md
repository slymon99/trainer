# Fuelling

On and off the bike. The training plan is in [`../plan.md`](../plan.md); this is the
eating that supports it.

Built around the actual constraints: **weekday sessions are early morning, indoor,
before a 5-day commute into midtown Manhattan.** Breakfast gets bought on the way to
work — a bagel shop or a food cart. Lunch is a midtown bowl place. Dinner is cooked
at home, so dinner is the meal that gets to fix whatever the day missed.

Portions are for **72 kg**. Macros are typical label and menu values — accurate
enough to order from, not accurate enough to audit against.

---

## The daily numbers

**Protein is fixed. Carbohydrate moves with the day. Fat fills the rest.**

| Day | Ride | Carb | Protein | Fat | ≈ kcal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Off, or 40 min recovery | 5 g/kg — **360 g** | **≥140 g** | 70–90 g | 2,600–2,800 |
| **Tue** | Sub-threshold, ~85 min | 6–7 g/kg — **430–500 g** | **≥140 g** | 70–90 g | 3,100–3,400 |
| **Wed** | 70 min easy + sprints | 5 g/kg — **360 g** | **≥140 g** | 70–90 g | 2,600–2,900 |
| **Thu** | Sweet spot, ~85 min | 6–7 g/kg — **430–500 g** | **≥140 g** | 70–90 g | 3,100–3,400 |
| **Fri** | 60 min easy, or off | 5 g/kg — **360 g** | **≥140 g** | 70–90 g | 2,600–2,900 |
| **Sat** | 2–2.5 h outdoor | 7 g/kg — **500 g** | **≥140 g** | 80–90 g | 3,400–3,600 |
| **Sun** | 3–3.5 h + blocks | 8–9 g/kg — **575–650 g** | **≥140 g** | 80–90 g | 3,900–4,300 |

**Under Variant B** (Thursday dropped, Sunday is a hard group ride): Thursday and
Saturday take the easy-day column, Sunday keeps its own, and the Monday after — a
full day off in the plan — still takes the Monday column, not less.

Four things about how to read this table:

**Protein doesn't move.** 140 g is ~1.9 g/kg, and it's the same on a rest day as on
Sunday. Adaptation is protein synthesis running for 24–48 h after a session, so the
day *after* the stimulus is not the day to cut it. Split it roughly 30–40 g across
four eating occasions rather than backloading it onto dinner — that spread matters
more than the total does once the total is adequate.

**On-bike carbohydrate counts toward the daily total.** Sunday's 80–90 g/h over
3.25 h is 260–290 g — most of the way to the Wednesday target before a single meal.
This is why the big days look enormous and don't feel it.

**Calories are the output, not the input.** Don't count them. Hit the carb and
protein numbers, let fat float in the 70–90 g band, and let the scale be the
instrument: `plan.md` commits to 72 kg with no restriction during build blocks, so a
drift down means eat more, not that something is working.

**Fat has a floor, not a target.** Below about 60 g/day sits hormone production and
fat-soluble vitamin absorption. It is the macro to trim when a day runs long, but
only down to that floor.

## On the bike

| Ride | Carbohydrate |
|---|---|
| Easy, under ~90 min | Nothing needed |
| Quality session, 60–90 min | 40–60 g/h — protects the last reps |
| 90 min – 2.5 h | ~60 g/h |
| 3–4 h, and camp days | **80–90 g/h.** Needs a glucose+fructose mix; single-source absorption tops out near 60 g/h |
| After anything hard or long | ~80–90 g carb + ~25 g protein within the hour |

The Sunday session and the long weekend rides work *because* they land on depleted
glycogen — but that state comes from the duration in front of them, not from
under-eating. Riding them under-fuelled just means riding them badly.

**Sodium indoors: 500–800 mg/h**, more in the summer. An 85-minute indoor session in
a New York August is a high-sweat session even with two fans, and the plan's heat
derate covers the watts but not the salt.

## The weekday morning

### Before — the pop tart is correct

Two pastries is ~76 g carb, ~4 g protein, low fat, low fibre. For a 30-minute window
that is close to the ideal pre-ride food: high glycaemic, empties fast, won't sit on
your stomach through 3×15 at 90–93%. **Don't replace it with oatmeal, eggs, or
anything with fibre or fat at that timing** — those are better foods that are worse
here.

- **Tue/Thu: eat both pastries.**
- **Wed/Fri: one is enough**, and nothing at all is fine on a 60-minute easy ride.

### During — this is the highest-leverage item on the page

You arrive overnight-fasted with ~76 g on board for an 85-minute session carrying
45+ minutes of hard work. Liver glycogen is at its daily low. **40–60 g/h in the
bottle on Tue/Thu is not optional on a morning session** — it is the difference
between the third interval holding 90–93% and the third interval being a story about
how you felt tired. If any single thing here is currently missing, it's this one.

Nothing needed on Wednesday or Friday. The Wednesday sprints are neuromuscular and
cost essentially no glycogen.

### After — the commute meal *is* the recovery meal

It lands 20–45 minutes post-session, which is the window `plan.md` asks for. On
Tue/Thu that meal has a job: **80–90 g carb and 25–30 g protein.** On Wed/Fri it has
no job beyond being breakfast.

The urgency is real here specifically because the session was near-fasted and lunch
is 4 hours out. On a fed afternoon ride it would matter much less.

## Breakfast — the bagel shop and the cart

| # | Build | Carb | Protein | Fat | kcal | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Bagel with egg and cheese + banana** | 97 | 29 | 17 | 655 | **Tue/Thu default** |
| **2** | **Bagel with lox**, 1 tbsp cream cheese + 8 oz OJ | 95 | 26 | 8 | 580 | Tue/Thu |
| **3** | **Cart egg-and-cheese on a roll** + Greek yogurt + banana | 73 | 34 | 18 | 595 | Tue/Thu, bagel line too long |
| **4** | **Muffin + Greek yogurt** | 68 | 22 | 20 | 540 | Cart-only fallback |
| **5** | **Bagel with cream cheese** + coffee | 70 | 14 | 10 | 450 | **Wed/Fri** |

**The egg is the entire point of the order on Tue/Thu.** A bagel with cream cheese is
~70 g carb and about **4 g of protein** — it hits the carb target and misses the
protein one by more than 20 g. That's build #5, and it's a Wednesday breakfast, not a
Tuesday one.

**The muffin is the weakest of the three vendors, and it's weakest exactly on the
days that matter.** A cart muffin runs ~62 g carb, ~7 g protein, ~20 g fat — the fat
slows the carbohydrate down and the protein isn't close. It's a fine Wednesday
breakfast and a poor Tuesday one.

**The fix for both: keep plain Greek yogurt or a shaker of whey at your desk.** One
tub is 15 g of protein and turns any cart-only morning into an adequate one. This is
the single habit that makes the whole breakfast problem go away, because it stops
depending on what's open.

Don't get the bagel scooped.

## Lunch — midtown bowls

**Bowl places default to a desk-worker's macros: ~500 kcal, 30 g carb, 30 g protein.**
That is a well-built lunch for someone who didn't ride 85 minutes at sub-threshold
this morning. Four changes turn it into one that works:

1. **Grain base, not greens.** Rice, wild rice, farro, quinoa. Ask for double base.
2. **Double the protein on quality and weekend days.** A single serving is enough on
   easy and rest days — breakfast and dinner already carry ~110 g between them, and
   doubling at lunch as well crowds out the carbohydrate inside the same calories.
   The daily 140 g floor holds either way; this is about where it comes from.
3. **Add a carb-dense topping** — sweet potato, corn, chickpeas, lentils, plantain,
   a side pita.
4. **Leave the dressing and the avocado alone.** Fat isn't the problem here.

| Build | Carb | Protein | Fat | kcal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm grain bowl** — double rice base, double chicken, roasted sweet potato, chickpeas, ½ avocado, dressing | 114 | 66 | 31 | 1,090 |
| **Mediterranean** — rice + lentil base, double grilled chicken, hummus, tzatziki, corn and cabbage, side pita | 114 | 72 | 22 | 970 |
| **Poke** — double rice, double salmon, edamame, seaweed, avocado, spicy mayo | 101 | 62 | 36 | 1,030 |
| **Easy-day version** — single protein, single base, skip the avocado | 108 | 41 | 15 | 750 |

**If the only option is a chopped salad place**, a greens-base bowl lands around
25 g carb and 35 g protein however you build it. That's not a failure — it just means
dinner is carrying about 150 g more carbohydrate than usual, so plan the evening
around pasta or rice rather than around salmon and vegetables.

| | Carb | Protein | Fat | kcal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oatmeal, 80 g dry, milk, banana, scoop of whey | 85 | 30 | 8 | 550 |
| Easy-day bowl, single protein | 108 | 41 | 15 | 750 |

**Monday is the day this gets got wrong.** It follows a 3–3.5 h Sunday with sweet
spot blocks in the final 45 minutes, and full glycogen resynthesis from that takes
well past 24 h. "Rest day, so eat less" would land the week's largest cut on the day
carrying the week's largest repayment — and it would directly contradict *no caloric
deficit during build blocks*. **Monday is an easy day's eating with no ride in it,
not a low day.**

Add fat if 2,600 kcal on a no-ride day feels short; the carb and protein numbers are
the ones holding.

## Weekends and camp

Saturday and Sunday are mostly an on-bike problem — the table above governs, and
Sunday's 260–290 g of in-ride carbohydrate does most of the daily work. Three notes:

- **Breakfast before a long ride:** 100–150 g carb, 2–3 h out if the timing allows,
  low fat and low fibre. If it's an early start, the pop tart approach scales — eat
  what you can 30 min out and move the rest into the first hour on the bike.
- **Sunday's blocks land in the final 45 minutes**, so the carb intake in hours 1–2
  is what pays for them. Front-load rather than rationing.
- **The Labor Day trip** (Fri 4 – Sun 6 Sep, [`block-1.md`](block-1.md)) runs the
  camp-day numbers all three days. Three consecutive days is where under-fuelling
  compounds, and the deficit shows up on day 3 as flat legs that read like fatigue
  and aren't.

## The rules that override everything above

**No caloric deficit during build blocks.** It compromises the exact adaptations
being chased, and this is a watts goal, not a weight goal.

**Rest days keep their protein and most of their carbohydrate.** See Monday.

**A hard session poorly fuelled is not a hard session.** `plan.md` reads normal HR
with high RPE as glycogen depletion, and the prescribed response is to cut the set a
rep short and eat more — not to lower the target. Check the fuelling before
concluding anything about fitness from a session that went badly.

**Don't train a weight change and a power goal at once.** If 72 kg starts drifting,
that's a fuelling problem to fix, not a result.

## Assumptions

1. **Weekday sessions are early morning, before the commute.** Inferred from a
   30-minute pre-ride window and buying breakfast on the way to work. If they're
   actually evenings, the breakfast section becomes an ordinary meal, dinner becomes
   the recovery meal, and the 30-minute pre-ride food moves to mid-afternoon —
   everything else holds.
2. **72 kg**, from `plan.md`, and stable.
3. **Non-exercise expenditure ~2,450–2,500 kcal** — desk job with a walking NYC
   commute. If that's off by 300 either way, the calorie column moves and the carb
   and protein columns don't.
4. **Ride energy costs are modelled from typical power for each session**, not from
   measured kJ. The store carries actual kJ per activity; worth checking the estimates
   against a few weeks of real files if the weight doesn't hold.
5. **No supplements, allergies, or restrictions** are accounted for, because none are
   recorded anywhere in the plan.
